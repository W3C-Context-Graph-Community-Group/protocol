# Meaning: temperature

**Asserter:** [Ron's system](https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/demo/systems/ron)

## Definition

Indoor air temperature measured by a ceiling-mounted HVAC sensor in Building 7, Room 301.

The sensor reports the ambient air temperature of the room, sampled every 60 seconds. The value represents the most recent reading.

## What this does NOT mean

- Not body temperature
- Not outdoor temperature
- Not water or process temperature
- Not CPU or equipment temperature
