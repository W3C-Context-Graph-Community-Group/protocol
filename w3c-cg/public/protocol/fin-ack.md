# protocol/fin-ack

**Teardown acknowledgment**

- **State transition:** `CLOSING → CLOSED`
- **Key:** `https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/protocol/fin-ack`
- **Value:** Acknowledgment of the closing system

A closed boundary may be reopened by initiating a new handshake. Comparing old and new evaluations reveals drift.

Part of the [Context Graph Protocol](https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/).
