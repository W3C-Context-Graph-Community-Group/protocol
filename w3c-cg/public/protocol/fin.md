# protocol/fin

**Teardown initiation**

- **State transition:** `ESTABLISHED → CLOSING`
- **Key:** `https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/protocol/fin`
- **Value:** Reason for closing (e.g., `reason:evaluation-complete`)

Either system may initiate teardown. Previous evaluation results remain in the graph.

Part of the [Context Graph Protocol](https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/).
