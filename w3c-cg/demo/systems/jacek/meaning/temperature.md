# Meaning: temperature

**Source:** [Jacek's system](https://w3c-cg.github.io/context-graph/demo/systems/jacek)

## Definition

Patient body temperature, oral reading, taken by nursing staff during routine vitals check.

The value represents the most recent measurement recorded in the patient's electronic health record.

## What this does NOT mean

- Not room or ambient air temperature
- Not outdoor temperature
- Not equipment temperature
